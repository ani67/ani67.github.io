In early 2025, I worked as the product designer alongside the CTO and three developers building a chat-based AI video creation platform.

The team was small, which meant my scope went well beyond end to end design: AI workflow, prompt engineering, running evals, fine-tuning the prompting system, user research, support handling, Mixpanel. This was one of the initial versions that went live in June 2025.

![Frameo.ai, 2025](https://res.cloudinary.com/duw0custw/image/upload/v1773772895/01_twuv4o.png "Frameo.ai, 2025")

Home page, describe your story, pick a style, hit create.

![Frameo.ai, 2025](https://res.cloudinary.com/duw0custw/image/upload/v1773764604/16_yxbjdj.png "Frameo.ai, 2025")

Adaptive UI on the left — script, characters, shots, preview, timeline, all on demand.

![Frameo.ai, 2025](https://res.cloudinary.com/duw0custw/image/upload/v1773764604/15_h0ggvu.png "Frameo.ai, 2025")

Video editing surface that pops up from the bottom of the preview window.

![Frameo.ai, 2025](https://res.cloudinary.com/duw0custw/image/upload/v1773764604/13_h1qgda.png "Frameo.ai, 2025")

Worked seamlessly on mobile too.

This version got our first subscription. A lot has changed since — the product, the experiments, the direction. More on that soon.
