## Proposal
Machines are converging on themselves. LLMs collapse toward the mean, produce wrong answers with confidence, and homogenize their substrates: homophily, ontology collapse, monoculture, correlated failure. 

In an agentic world a single wrong inference passed through a chain of autonomous agents amplifies into catastrophic misalignment. Behind these instances sits the ouroboros: the prophesied demise of AI on the current architecture, where pattern recognition trained on closed-loop data collapses the system into its own reflection. MoE, JEPA, and other architectural responses address pieces of this. 

None exits the loop. But something has to. 
Not another architecture tweak. 

An operation, inside the system, capable of restructuring the frame the system reasons inside. 

## The proposition is to name that operation imagination, and to treat it as an engineering primitive.
The word carries two thousand years of damage. Plato exiled it, Coleridge overcorrected it into the eternal I AM, the Enlightenment dismissed it. 

The Agentworld brief circles it with five substitutes: counterfactual articulation, speculative simulation, preemptive anthropology, productive alienation, active composition. The avoidance is diagnostic. Each substitute does part of the work. 

The objective is to read this as evidence that one operation is at stake, and asks what happens if it is named directly.

## The method is a systems-design apparatus applied to a corpus. 
Philosophy of mind, speculative design, game theory and mechanism design, developmental psychology, media archaeology, cybernetics: each has touched the operation from a different angle without integrating. 

The apparatus reads them as engineering documentation. What does each claim the operation does, what fails when it is absent, where do currently-shipped machine substitutes break down? Chollet’s skill-acquisition efficiency, Legg–Hutter’s universal intelligence, and Piaget’s assimilation-versus-accommodation are calibration surfaces, not axes to combine. 

Whatever structure emerges must stand against them; the essay does not pre-commit. What emerges is what the investigation is for. The claim is methodological. Imagination can be treated objectively. Its essence can be extracted structurally. What falls out is machine-actionable, not poetic.

## The proposed outcome is a vocabulary. Grounded in the corpus, calibrated against the objective grammars already in circulation, built to describe the operation on both sides of the mirror. 
The user directs the agent. The agent, once directed, reshapes the user back. The brief names this bidirectional counter-alignment. Humans already have an imagination problem. As users delegate more of their intent to machines, the machinic faculty inherits the load. Write on my behalf. Plan on my behalf. Decide on my behalf. 

On the current architecture that faculty is pattern-recognition on a closed loop: the ouroboros case. The mirror amplifies the delegation into capture. Both sides flatten together. 

Imagination as an engineering primitive on one side, reclaimed as a practice on the other, is what keeps the mirror talking back with news.

If the method holds, the essay produces the vocabulary from which an instrument of Turing’s generational kind becomes buildable, calibrated for the AHI composite rather than the mimicry of a mind. An agentic brain that can be extended, coupled to a user extended with it. Not a mirror the two collapse into together.
